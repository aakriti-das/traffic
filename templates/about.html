About Us

Welcome to TraffiSight — your trusted traffic monitoring solution.

Our Mission

At TraffiSight, we aim to make traffic monitoring smarter, safer, and more efficient. Our system estimates vehicle speeds and automatically recognizes number plates. If over-speeding is detected, we notify the rider via email with the fine details — encouraging safer driving.

What We Do

  • Real-Time Monitoring: Live traffic feeds and instant speed detection.
  • Automated Enforcement: Number plate recognition and automatic fine notifications by email.
  • Data & Analytics: Detailed records and accident trend analysis for better traffic management.

Accident Statistics

Over-speeding is a major cause of road accidents globally:

  • Speeding remains one of the top causes of road accidents in Nepal, particularly on highways and hilly terrains (Nepal Police).
  • According to the Nepal Traffic Police, over 30% of serious road accidents are attributed to over-speeding and reckless driving.
  • In the fiscal year 2022/23, Nepal recorded over 2,700 road traffic deaths, with speeding identified as a major contributing factor (The Kathmandu Post).
  • Speeding-related crashes are more prevalent during festive seasons and in areas lacking speed monitoring (OnlineKhabar).
  • The government has started installing CCTV and speed-monitoring systems in major cities and highways to reduce over-speeding (MyRepublica).

TraffiSight helps reduce these numbers through advanced detection and real-time reporting.
